What we hold to

Four things we don't negotiate on.

01

Quote it properly

An itemised scope before work starts. If we can't estimate something confidently, we say so rather than padding it quietly.

02

Bad news early

If a date is at risk or a material isn't available, you hear it that week — not at handover when nothing can be done about it.

03

Finish the last 5%

The snag list, the documentation, the trim that never got fitted. Projects are judged on the ending, so we close it out.

04

No lock-in

Code, drawings, specs and accounts are yours. Stay with us because the work is good, not because leaving is painful.
