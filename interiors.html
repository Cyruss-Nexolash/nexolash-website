Questions

What clients ask before signing.

Do you take small jobs, or only full fit-outs?

Both. Plenty of our work is a single trade — a false ceiling in one hall, a wardrobe, a kitchen countertop. There's no minimum you need to hit before we'll come and look at it.

How is the quote calculated?

Item by item, in a BOQ: each element with its quantity, material specification and rate. Ceilings and flooring are usually per square foot, joinery per running foot or per unit, stone per square foot of slab. Nothing is bundled into a vague lump sum.

Can we supply our own materials?

Yes. Some clients buy their own granite or plywood and use us for labour and supervision only. We'll quote it either way and tell you honestly where our sourcing saves you money and where it doesn't.

How long does a typical project take?

A single false ceiling is usually a few days to a week. A kitchen or wardrobe set runs two to four weeks. A full apartment fit-out is generally six to twelve weeks depending on scope. You get dated milestones in the proposal.

What happens if something goes wrong after handover?

Workmanship is warranted, and hardware and materials carry their manufacturers' warranties. Call us and we come back — that's the whole point of using a team that's still around next year.

Which areas of Coimbatore and Tamil Nadu do you cover?

We are based in Coimbatore and work throughout Tamil Nadu with our own crews and trade partners. Tell us the location when you get in touch and we'll confirm coverage and what a site visit would involve.

Send us the space.

Share your floor plan, a few photos, or just the address and what you have in mind. We'll set up a site visit and come back with a detailed BOQ — no charge for the estimate.
