Questions

The things people ask first.

What does a project cost?

It depends entirely on scope, so we don't publish a price list we'd have to caveat. What we do is give you a fixed number after discovery — a small automation and a full platform are different orders of magnitude, and you should know which one you're buying before you commit.

How long does it take?

An AI workflow or automation is usually 3–8 weeks. An MVP or internal platform is typically 8–16 weeks. We give you milestone dates in the proposal and tell you early if anything is at risk of slipping.

Who owns the code?

You do — completely, from the first commit. The repository is yours, the infrastructure is in your accounts, and there is nothing proprietary of ours that you'd need to keep paying for.

Can you work with our existing developers?

Yes. We regularly join existing teams, pick up half-finished projects, or provide dedicated developers who work inside your process and tooling.

Do you maintain what you build?

Every project includes a warranty period. After that most clients move to a monthly retainer covering fixes, monitoring and a budget of hours for changes — but it's optional, and you can take it in-house whenever you like.

Is AI actually right for our problem?

Often it isn't, and we'll say so. Plenty of problems are better solved with a well-built form and a database than with a model. We'd rather tell you that in discovery than sell you something that disappoints.

Have a system that needs building?

Send us the problem — even roughly. We'll come back with an honest view of what it takes, what it costs, and whether it's worth doing at all.
